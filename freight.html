Data & Business Analytics Industry Project

This project occurred during my 4th term at SUTD (Jan 2025 – Apr 2025) under the Data and Business Analytics module.My teammates and I were tasked by the company to develop predictive freight models for different trade lanes with me mainly helping on developing and running the necessary R scripts. At the end of the project, my teammates and I were able to deliver the 4 models with additional research information to the company. We also got to present some of our findings to the public during the DBA showcase

Skills Learnt

  • Technical: R Programming for the models and result display, Bloomberg for industry research and gathering of data, Excel for data work
  • Theoretical: Problems that come with data snooping, the logic behind selecting which models to use, data normalization & cleaning so that different raw numbers will not affect results
  • Soft: Gaining the confidence of voicing my opinions, built work ethic, learn how to better support my teammates if they need help in an area

Reflection

In the end, I thought the project was extremely successful. I picked up a lot of new tips and tricks regarding how to work with data and its relevant software, this was especially useful as the project happened during the first term of my major where I was still full of uncertainty and questions regarding a larger scale analysis project. The lessons of this project helped built my foundation which would be useful for me in my later modules.
